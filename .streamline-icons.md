## Streamline assets

This project's icons and illustrations come from Streamline, through the Streamline MCP. Pick by type and role:

- Icons, from the Phosphor Family (`phosphor`), in `assets/streamline/`:
  - `phosphor-fill` (Phosphor Fill): all UI icons replacing emoji (robot, thumbs-up, warning, danger, chili, smile, sad, sick, sprout, clipboard, clock, plate, bolt, info, celebrate)

If something is missing, use the closest match in the same Set.
